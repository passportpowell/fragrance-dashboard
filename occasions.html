Where & When to Wear

WHEN TO WEAR

Pick the right scent for gym, running, walking, work, and dates

Body heat, sweat, and room size change how fragrances smell. Here is what to wear for workouts, casual days, office hours, and nights out.

Workouts & Training

Gym & Workouts

Why this works

When you work out, your skin heats up fast. Heavy vanilla, tobacco, or oud scents will project too aggressively, choke people out on nearby machines, and give you a headache.

For the gym, use light, airy molecular scents (Iso E Super and Ambroxan) or fresh citrus aquatics. They smell clean and fresh as you sweat without ever feeling overpowering.

Spray Tip for Gym

Routine: 2 sprays behind the ears or directly on your gym t-shirt. Never spray your chest before heavy cardio.

High-Heat Cardio

Running & Outdoor Sports

Why this works

Outdoor running combines fast wind airflow with skin warmth. Light citrus and woody molecular notes project cleanly in open air without getting thick or cloying.

Spray Tip for Running

Routine: 2 sprays on the back of your neck so the breeze carries the scent behind you while you run.

Work & Office

Office & Everyday Work

Why this works

In meeting rooms and closed offices, you want a scent that smells clean, masculine, and well-groomed without choking colleagues. Crisp bergamot, incense, cedarwood, and lavender are the standard for professional settings.

Spray Tip for Work

Routine: 3 sprays maximum (behind each ear and 1 on shirt collar).

Black Tie & Celebrations

Weddings & Special Events

Why this works

Formal suits, celebrations, and banquet halls require dignified, high-concentration sillage that lasts all day and night. Rich vanilla, honey tobacco, warm spices, and golden amber create a memorable, elegant presence.

Spray Tip for Formal Events

Routine: 3 sprays (1 behind each ear, 1 on suit lapel). Projects softly throughout long celebrations.
