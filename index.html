Maison Philosophy

THE SCENT CODEX & CRAFTSMANSHIP

The principles of sillage architecture, clone oxygenation, and concentration dynamics.

I.

The Anti-Noseblind Law

Spraying the front of your chest or throat directly exposes your olfactory receptors to rising volatile fumes, inducing olfactory fatigue (anosmia) within 20 minutes. You believe your fragrance has vanished while projecting a heavy cloud onto everyone else.

The Voj Protocol: Apply exclusively behind each earlobe and across the nape of the neck. Movement creates an intoxicating trailing sillage wake behind you without fatiguing your own senses.

II.

Artisanal Maturation

Freshly compounded perfume oils and clones from houses like Perfume Parlour undergo active molecular bonding. Upon receiving a new flacon, spray 5 to 10 sprays to introduce oxygen into the headspace of the bottle.

Store in a dark, temperate drawer for 2 to 6 weeks. The sharp alcohol opening softens, allowing deep resins, vanilla, and woods to meld into a seamless, velvety drydown.

III.

The Concentration Spectrum

Eau de Toilette (5–15% Oil): Sharp expansive projection ideal for warm athletics.
Eau de Parfum (15–20% Oil): The golden mean of 8-hour versatility.
Extrait de Parfum (20–40% Oil): Narcotic, resinous density lasting 12+ hours.
Pure Perfume Oil (100%): Intimate warmth and the ideal Middle Eastern base.
