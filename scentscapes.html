OLFACTORY SCENTSCAPES

Les Atmosphères de Vie & Contextes Cinématiques

Fragrance is never worn in a vacuum. Kinetic skin heat, ambient humidity, heart rate, and emotional tension dictate how volatile molecules project. Discover our five calibrated atmospheric realms engineered for specific physical rituals.

Realm I // Athletics & Kinetic Motion

High-Heat Motion & Athletics (Running & Sport)

Diffusion Dynamics

During high-cadence running or intensive gym training, skin temperature surges past 34°C, accelerating volatile molecular evaporation by over 200%. Dense ambers and heavy ouds turn cloying and induce immediate nausea.

This realm utilizes sparkling solar mandarins, high-diffusion mineral musks, and Iso E Super molecular primers that create a weightless, invigorating cocoon that thrives under heavy perspiration without olfactory fatigue.

Voj Athletic Application Blueprint

Step 1: 2 sprays behind the ears/nape only (never the chest).
Step 2: 1 mist directly across athletic apparel collar for ambient evaporation as you run.

Realm II // Contemplative Motion

The Open-Air Stroll (Walking & Weekend Strolls)

Atmospheric Interaction

Outdoor walking subjects perfume to moving air currents. Crisp autumn breezes disperse delicate top notes quickly while pulling forward comforting woody backbones, sweet bourbon vanillas, and aged mahogany accords.

These compositions leave a gentle, aristocratic sillage wake trailing behind you that enchants passersby without ever feeling intrusive in public parks or bustling streets.

Voj Walking Protocol

Step 1: 2 sprays behind each earlobe.
Step 2: 1 spray across the nape of the neck.
Step 3: 1 spray on outerwear (coat or scarf).

Realm III // Nocturnal Intimacy

Intimate Nightfall & Date Nights

Chemistry of Seduction

Date night perfumery operates in the close-encounter sphere (within 50 cm). Beast-mode freshness feels aggressive in candlelit lounges. Instead, resinous depth, boozy Martinique rum, bitter griotte cherry liqueur, and golden tobacco create an irresistible magnetic pull.

Voj Date Night Protocol

Step 1: 2 sprays behind the ears.
Step 2: 1 spray at the base of the throat for intimate close encounters.

Realm IV // Executive Presence

The Boardroom & Formal Sovereign

Corporate Authority

In professional negotiations and corporate environments, your fragrance must project competence, hygiene, and calm executive authority. Overpowering sweet florals signal informality; instead, sharp Italian bergamot, smoky incense, and French lavender fougères establish effortless respect.

Voj Executive Protocol

Step 1: 2 sprays behind the ears.
Step 2: 1 spray across the back of the neck under shirt collar.
