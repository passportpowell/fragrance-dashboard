Tips & Best Practices

FRAGRANCE GUIDE

Practical advice on spraying, bottle care, maceration, and concentrations

Personal guidance for trying, wearing and caring for fragrances. Record your own experience; recommendations and estimates are provisional.

Personal guidance. No timed performance guarantees or scientific verification are implied.

1.

Considerate application

Start lightly and consider fragrance-free spaces and nearby people. Your own perception can change during wear, so avoid repeatedly adding more simply because you cannot smell it. Follow product directions and stop use if irritation occurs.

2.

Give new bottles a fair trial

Try a new bottle on separate days before judging it. Resting a bottle is a personal practice, not a guarantee of improved strength or smoothness. There is no need to waste sprays or deliberately introduce air. Record your own impressions.

3.

Read the label, then observe

Extrait, Eau de Parfum, body spray and daily fragrance are product labels. They do not establish a universal oil percentage or wear time. Performance and volume estimates remain provisional until you confirm them.

4.

Keep bottles protected

Follow manufacturer storage directions. Keep caps fitted and bottles upright, away from direct sunlight and excessive heat. Store somewhere stable and safe rather than a hot car or sunny window.

5.

Try layering as an experiment

Try each fragrance alone first, then test a small combination. This dashboard cannot establish that molecular-style fragrances bind to skin, prevent noseblindness or extend longevity. Pairing scores are heuristics based on tags and accords, not laboratory measurements.
