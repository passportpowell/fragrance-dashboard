Tips & Best Practices

FRAGRANCE GUIDE

Practical advice on spraying, bottle care, maceration, and concentrations

Simple, proven tips to get the best performance and longest life out of your bottles without going noseblind.

1.
Spraying Technique

How to Avoid Going Noseblind (The Voj Routine)

Your brain tunes out constant smells so it can detect new scents in your environment. If you spray your chest, throat, or front of your neck, perfume fumes rise straight into your nose with every single breath. Within 15 to 20 minutes, your nose stops smelling it completely (habituation).

When you think your fragrance has disappeared and spray more, you choke out everyone in the room while smelling nothing yourself.

Where to spray instead:
• Behind each ear: 1 spray behind each earlobe.
• Back of the neck: 1 spray right across the nape of your neck.
• Clothing (Optional): 1 spray on your jacket collar or shirt cuffs.

Why this works: When you walk or turn your head, you leave a trail behind you that other people smell, but your nose isn't constantly blasted with fumes.
2.
Bottle Care

Letting Bottles Settle (Maceration for Clones)

Independent clone houses like Perfume Parlour, French Avenue, and Paris Corner often bottle their perfumes fresh. Right out of the shipping box, the alcohol and fragrance oils haven't fully blended yet. This is why a new bottle can smell harsh or medicinal at first.

The 3-Step Routine:
1. Spray 5 to 10 sprays into the air to clear the atomizer and let fresh oxygen into the top of the bottle.
2. Put the cap back on and place the bottle in a dark, cool drawer or cupboard.
3. Let it rest for 3 to 6 weeks.

Result: The harsh alcohol smell fades away, and deeper notes like vanilla, tobacco, rum, and amber become significantly smoother and stronger.
3.
Strength Guide

Extrait vs EDP vs Body Sprays: Understanding Concentrations

A common myth is that Extrait de Parfum always projects louder into a room than lighter concentrations. In reality, alcohol is what evaporates quickly and pushes fragrance molecules into the air. Higher alcohol content gives wider projection; higher perfume oil concentration creates longer-lasting skin scent and richer sillage density.

• Extrait de Parfum (20–40% Perfume Oil): The most concentrated formulation (e.g. After Effect, Wood Miracle, Intense Aroma Exploding). Sits closer to skin with dense sillage, lasting 10–14+ hours. Ideal for cool weather, date nights, and formal celebrations.
• Eau de Parfum (15–20% Perfume Oil): The balanced golden standard (e.g. Navy Intense, Voux Elegante, Noir Extreme, Herb Garden). Offers strong projection for 3–4 hours and lasts 7–10 hours. Perfect for work, daily signatures, and evenings.
• All-Over Body Sprays & Daily Pumps (3–8% Oil): Aerosols (Tom Ford Oud Wood, Bath & Body Works Mahogany Woods) and precision pumps (Lynx Signature Oud & Dark Vanilla). Designed for post-shower freshness, gym bags, and active lifestyles where heavy perfumes would feel suffocating.
• Single-Molecule Isolates: Pure synthetic aroma-chemicals (Iso E Super, Ambroxan) suspended in alcohol. Adaptable fixatives that project transparently and amplify companion fragrances.
4.
Storage Tips

Where to Store Your Bottles

Two things destroy fragrance oils: heat fluctuations and direct sunlight. Storing bottles on a bathroom counter or a sunny windowsill will ruin the fresh citrus top notes within months.

Storage Rules:
• Keep bottles away from hot bathrooms and shower steam.
• Keep bottles out of direct sunlight.
• Store them upright in a bedroom wardrobe, drawer, or dark cupboard at normal room temperature.
5.
Scent Boosters

Using Molecule 01 & 02 as Transparent Primers

Single-molecule fragrances like Molecule 01 (Particle / Iso E Super) and Molecule 02 (Second Particle / Ambroxan) interact with your skin's natural warmth and lipids.

Instead of competing with other perfumes, they act as transparent diffusive anchors that hold onto your fragrance notes and create an alluring sillage trail without olfactory fatigue (anosmia).

How to use them as a primer:
1. Apply 2 sprays of Molecule 01 or 02 on pulse points (base of neck or wrists).
2. Wait 30 seconds for the alcohol carrier to evaporate.
3. Mist your main fragrance directly on top or on shirt collar.

Result: Your main fragrance lasts longer on skin and projects in waves throughout the day.
